# Code of conduct

keqdroid is used by people in different countries, with different languages and levels of experience. This code sets the rules for everyone who takes part in the project: users, contributors and the maintainer.

## Where it applies

- this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews and comments;
- the [Telegram chat](https://t.me/keqdroid);
- any other place where someone acts on behalf of the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be polite and patient. Many people come here with a connection problem and no technical background.
- Write in Russian or English, whichever is easier for you.
- Criticize code and decisions, not people.
- Before posting a log or a configuration, remove subscription links, UUIDs, passwords and keys from it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- insults, harassment, threats and personal attacks;
- discrimination based on nationality, ethnicity, language, gender, sexual orientation, religion, age, disability or level of experience;
- sexual content and unwanted sexual attention;
- publishing someone else's private information without their permission, including IP addresses, subscription links and server configurations;
- advertising VPN services, subscriptions or servers, referral links and spam. Discussing how a provider's panel or subscription works with keqdroid is welcome;
- passing off a modified or repackaged build as an official keqdroid release;
- public posts about unfixed vulnerabilities. Report them as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md);
- flooding, deliberately derailing discussions, and continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a violation to lenorakirechaeva@gmail.com. Include a link to the message or a screenshot. The name of the person who reports is not disclosed without their consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainer, [Lemonochka](https://github.com/Lemonochka), reviews every report and decides on the response. Messages that break this code can be edited, hidden or deleted. Depending on how serious the violation is and whether it is repeated, the response is:

1. a warning, private or public;
2. a temporary ban from the repository or the chat;
3. a permanent ban.

Threats, publishing private information and passing off modified builds as official releases can lead to a permanent ban without a warning.
